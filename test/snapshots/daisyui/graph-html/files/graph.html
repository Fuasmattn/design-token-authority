    What is chain depth?

    Chain depth measures how many alias references a token follows before reaching a concrete value. It shows the indirection layers in your token architecture.

    Depth 0 — raw value, no aliases
    Primitives/Colors/Blue/500 = #3b82f6

    Depth 1 — one alias hop
    Brand/Primary → Primitives/Colors/Blue/500

    Depth 2 — two alias hops
    Button/Background → Brand/Primary → Blue/500

    Depth 3+ — deep chains (may indicate over-abstraction)

    In a healthy 3-layer architecture (Primitives → Brand → ScreenType), most tokens are depth 1–2. Depth 3+ may signal unnecessary indirection.
