Components use only the **semantic** tokens. Each one aliases a primitive per theme; the arrow shows the alias. Swatches are drawn with the CSS variables `dta build` writes, so what you see is the built output.

Rules from the system: every text pair reaches 7:1 in both themes (`fg-*` on `bg-*`, `action-fg` on `action-bg`, `action-text` and `signal-text` on the canvas and their tints, the terminal's four colours on `terminal-bg`); a test in the repository checks them. Text on `signal-bg` is always `signal-fg` (dark), never white, and that pair is the one exception at 4.5:1: the redline label. `signal` is never used for errors. `ink-600`, `graphite-400` and `paper-500` are decorative and not for text.

The `status-*` group holds the [status colours](/design-system/status-colours/): `status-{success,warning,error}-text` for words (7:1 on `bg-canvas`, `bg-surface`, `bg-subtle` and its own subtle), `-marker` for marker shapes and banner outlines (3:1 on the same, not for text: `sage-600`, `ochre-600` and `crimson-600` in Light), `-subtle` for tints. Their primitives are `sage`, `ochre` and `crimson`. `terminal-success` stays the terminal's own token: the terminal is dark in both themes.

The `well-*` group draws the landing page's gravity well: `well-line` is the mesh at the surface, `well-throat` what it darkens to toward the mouth, `well-mouth` the ground at the bottom (the other theme's paper) with a `well-glow` rim; the `well-object-*` triples are the lit, top and shaded faces of the cubes and spheres in orbit, `well-highlight` their specular point, `well-dust` the motes.
