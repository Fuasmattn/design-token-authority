DTA Drafting Table is the design system of this website, the `dta diff` reports and the playground. It looks like a drafting table: warm paper, blueprint blue, ink outlines and orange redlines that measure what changed. Its dark mode, Graphite, is the dark side of the same paper. Since the landing redesign the paper is one plain surface with a gravity well drawn on it: everything on the page gravitates towards the token.

It is also a demo of DTA itself: the tokens are DTA token files in `site/tokens`, linted with `dta lint` and built with `dta build` into the CSS this page uses. The token pages read the same files through `@dta/core`.

## Principles

1. **Measure, don't decorate.** The signature element is the redline: a `stroke-redline` line in `signal-bg` with a mono label naming the token it measures (`radius.lg · 18`). It points at real values, never at nothing.
2. **Two inks and a signal.** `fg-default` and `action-bg` carry the page. `signal-bg` appears only where something changed or is moving through the workflow. More than three orange marks on a screen is too many.
3. **Borders, not shadows.** Surfaces get a `stroke-hairline` in `border-default`; interactive elements a `stroke-ink` in `border-strong`.
4. **One surface.** The page is plain paper (`bg-canvas`), without grid lines. Grid paper (`bg-grid` at a `space-4` pitch on `bg-subtle`) appears only where something is literally being inspected: the redline stage and the card's stage variant. Never as a page, panel or section background (a test checks the components).
5. **The well is decoration.** The gravity well of the landing page (a polar mesh swirling into a hole, with cubes, spheres and dust in orbit) labels nothing and tells no story; the copy does. It is drawn from the `well-*` tokens, fades into the plain surface before any content starts, and stands still under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
6. **AAA.** Text reaches 7:1 on its ground in both themes (4.5:1 for large text, 24 px or 18.66 px bold), measured over the effective background; edges and the signal line reach 3:1. The one exception is the redline label (`signal-fg` on `signal-bg`, 5.7:1): no orange can be a 3:1 line on paper and carry 7:1 text at once. A test checks the token pairs; a browser test checks the rendered pages.
7. **Deterministic motion.** Motion shows process, not mood: redlines draw in, labels pop, the workflow packet travels the rail. All motion stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Voice

Plain, specific, calm. Short sentences. Name the thing: "1 rename: color.cta → color.action", not "Some changes were detected". Commands are shown as command chips you can copy (`$ npm create dta` with a copy icon that turns into a tick), the same component everywhere; the chip, not a blue button, is the call to action. Designers and engineers are addressed equally; never "simply" or "just".
