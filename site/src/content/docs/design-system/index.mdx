DTA Drafting Table is the design system of this website, the playground and the `dta diff` report: warm paper, blueprint blue, ink outlines and orange redlines. Graphite is its dark mode. Its tokens are DTA token files in `site/tokens`, linted and built with DTA.

## Principles

1. **Measure, don't decorate.** The redline (`stroke-redline` in `signal-bg`, with a mono label like `radius.lg · 18`) points at real values only.
2. **Two inks and a signal.** `fg-default` and `action-bg` carry the page. `signal-bg` only marks what changed or is moving; three orange marks per screen at most. Results use the [status colours](/design-system/status-colours/); `signal` never means an error or warning.
3. **Borders, not shadows.** A block that sits on the page (the workflow's pull request, the checks) is a well: `bg-subtle`, no border; the terminal is the one object on the paper. Surfaces that hold content of their own (cards, inputs, the docs' examples): `stroke-hairline` in `border-default`. Interactive elements: `stroke-ink` in `border-strong`. A rounded box has its outline on every side; a one-sided rule belongs to a square box. No faded or masked borders (`components.test.ts` checks).
4. **One surface.** The page is plain `bg-canvas`. Grid paper (`bg-grid`, `space-4` pitch, on `bg-subtle`) only where something is inspected: the redline stage and the card's stage variant (a test checks).
5. **The well is decoration.** The landing page's gravity well labels nothing, uses the `well-*` tokens, fades out before content and stands still under reduced motion.
6. **AAA.** Text 7:1 on its ground in both themes (4.5:1 for large text, 24 px or 18.66 px bold); edges and the signal line 3:1. Only exception: the redline label (`signal-fg` on `signal-bg`, 5.7:1). Tests check the token pairs and the rendered pages.
7. **Motion against change blindness.** Every change a person should notice is transitioned, on the motion tokens; no bounce. Under reduced motion changes are instant, but changed values are still highlighted. Rules: [Motion](/design-system/motion/).

## Voice

Plain, specific, calm. Name the thing: "1 rename: color.cta → color.action", not "Some changes were detected". Commands are copyable [command chips](/design-system/components/command-button/), not blue buttons. Never "simply" or "just".

## Patterns

- **Severity markers.** A word plus a shape in its [status colour](/design-system/status-colours/): error a filled square, warning an open ring, pass a dot. Colour never alone.
- **Examples stay blue and grey.** Example content never uses `signal` or the status colours ([rule](/design-system/status-colours/#examples-stay-blue-and-grey)).
- **Paths break between segments**, after `/` or `.`, through the [Token path](/design-system/components/token-path/) helper.
- **Status banner** for what stops the work (unreadable input, a failed build): the status tint inside a 2 px outline in its marker colour ([Findings](/design-system/components/findings/) `banner`).
- **A workspace, not a stacked page.** Input first ([Token input](/design-system/components/token-input/)); once loaded it collapses into a [Source bar](/design-system/components/source-bar/), with a [Summary line](/design-system/components/summary-line/) and [Tabs](/design-system/components/tabs/) for the views.
- **Side panel / bottom sheet.** Details open in the [Detail panel](/design-system/components/detail-panel/): beside the view from 1024 px, a bottom sheet below. Focus moves in and back; Escape closes. No modal dialogs for the views themselves.
- **A graph on any screen.** [Graph canvas](/design-system/components/graph-canvas/) on wide screens, [Graph list](/design-system/components/graph-list/) on phones, [Graph switcher](/design-system/components/graph-switcher/) for brand and breakpoint.
- **Files to take away.** Generated files are listed, not dumped ([Output files](/design-system/components/output-files/)), with Copy and Download per file and one "Download all"; the way back to the CLI is the [CLI hand-off](/design-system/components/cli-handoff/).
