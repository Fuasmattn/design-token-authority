A grid of [command chips](/design-system/components/command-button/), one muted line under each, no rules, no glyphs, no boxes. On the landing page it says what `dta` is made of without telling a story.

<Examples component="commands" />

## Guidelines

- One line per command, under thirteen words: what it does, not how.
- Only shipped commands (the landing page's honesty rule checks them).
- Three columns on desktop, two on tablets, a list on phones.
