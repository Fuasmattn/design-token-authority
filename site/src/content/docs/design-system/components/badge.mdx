A small upper-case label that qualifies what is next to it: **example** (example data), **coming** (not released yet) or **note** (a short hint).

<Examples component="badge" />

## Guidelines

- Badges qualify, chips describe: "coming" is a badge, "changed · 4.2%" a [chip](/design-system/components/chip/).
- Every number or command on the site that is not real today carries one.
- **Example** is an outline in `border-strong` with `fg-default` text, never `signal` or a status colour: orange would read as "changed".
- **Coming** and **note** are filled `action-subtle`.
