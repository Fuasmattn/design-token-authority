The [Graph canvas](/design-system/components/graph-canvas/)'s data as a list for phones: each layer a disclosure, every token with its alias chain and value. A row opens the token in the [Detail panel](/design-system/components/detail-panel/)'s bottom sheet.

## When to use

- In the [playground](/playground/)'s Graph view up to 760 px, instead of the canvas.

<Examples component="graph-list" />

## Guidelines

- **Layers** are native `details`/`summary` between hairline rules, at least 52 px tall: collection (mono kicker), mode and count, the worst finding as marker and word (`■ 1 error`, [Status colours](/design-system/status-colours/)), and a chevron. A file finding sits under the summary as a button to the Lint view.
- **Token rows** are buttons, at least 52 px: the full name in mono, then the chain and value (swatch for colours) in `fg-muted`, or why it doesn't resolve in `status-error-text`. A row with a finding ends in its marker and word.
- **Selection:** `aria-current` on `action-subtle`, text in `action-text`; markers keep their status colours. Moving the selection into a closed layer opens it; a layer the visitor opened is never closed.
- Paths break between segments ([Token path](/design-system/components/token-path/)); nothing scrolls sideways.
- **Motion:** layers grow open (`motion.duration.base`), selection tints over `fast`; at once under reduced motion ([Motion](/design-system/motion/)).
