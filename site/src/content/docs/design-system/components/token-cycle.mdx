A word under a redline whose tag names the type token that styles it. It cycles through a few styles, on click and every two seconds: the weight glides along the variable font's axis, a change to or from italic swaps under a short fade (italic is its own font file), the redline is drawn again and the token name pops in. Click the word in the examples to see it change.

<Examples component="token-cycle" />

## Guidelines

- One per page, in the hero: it shows what DTA is about, a token changing what you see.
- Three or four styles: weights within the font's range (400 to 700) and italic. The names on the tag are illustrative.
- Decorative: the word stays plain text, so a heading reads as written. Hovering pauses it; reduced motion turns the automatic change and the animations off. Weight glides and the redline redraws over `motion.duration.slow`; the tag pops over `base`.
- Stories set `autoplay={false}`, so screenshots are stable.
