Views of one thing, one at a time: the [playground](/playground/)'s Structure · Lint · Graph · Outputs. Only the selected panel is rendered.

## When to use

- When several views share one subject and stacking them would make a long page.
- Not for page navigation ([Nav](/design-system/components/nav/)) or a two-state choice inside a panel.

<Examples component="tabs" />

## Guidelines

- A `bg-subtle` well (`radius-md`, `space-1` padding); the selected tab on `bg-surface` with a `stroke-ink` outline in `border-strong`, the others in `fg-muted`. Label type, 44 px tall; a count after the label in regular weight (`Lint · 3`).
- **Keyboard** (WAI-ARIA tabs, automatic activation): only the selected tab is in the tab order; ←/→ select, Home/End go to the ends. `tablist` with a name, `tab` with `aria-selected` and `aria-controls`, one `tabpanel` labelled by its tab.
- The playground keeps the selected tab in the URL hash (`#lint`).
- **Phones:** below 560 px of container the tabs share the row equally; with `sticky` the bar stays on top on `bg-canvas` with a hairline under it.
- **Motion:** the selection indicator slides (`motion.duration.base`, `ease.standard`), the panel crossfades in a `dta-tabs` view transition, a changed count lights up ([Changed](/design-system/components/changed/)). Reduced motion: instant.
