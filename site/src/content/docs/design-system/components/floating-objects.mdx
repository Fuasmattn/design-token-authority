A few of the [gravity well](/design-system/components/gravity-well/)'s objects floating free behind the slot: two cubes, two spheres, four motes. Nothing holds them yet. The landing page ends with them around its final call.

<Examples component="floating-objects" />

## Guidelines

- Each object drifts on its own loop (9–14 s, alternating), cubes tumble (38–52 s), and each slides at its own depth (0.12–0.4) against the scroll: `translateY(d × depth)` where d is the section centre's distance from the viewport centre.
- No orbit rings, no mesh: the point is the contrast with the hero.
- Same object markup and tokens as the well. All still under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
