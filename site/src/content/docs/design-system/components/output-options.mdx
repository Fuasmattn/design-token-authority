The choices that shape what is built, in one row above the result: in the [playground](/playground/)'s Outputs view, which outputs `dta build` writes, with "Download all" at the end.

## When to use

- For a few independent on/off choices that change the result below them at once (no "Apply").
- Not for choosing a view ([Tabs](/design-system/components/tabs/)) or one value of many.

<Examples component="output-options" />

## Guidelines

- A labelled `role="group"` of native checkboxes in `action-bg`, each with a 44 px hit area.
- No card: a hairline `border-default` rule closes the row.
- Actions (at most one primary) sit at the end; below 560 px the choices form two columns and the actions get their own line.
- With nothing chosen, say what to do ("Choose at least one output to build."); don't disable the boxes.
