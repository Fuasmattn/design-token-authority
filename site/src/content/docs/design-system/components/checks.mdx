The checks of a pull request as flat rows on `bg-surface` with a hairline, and the approve action.

<Examples component="checks" />

## Guidelines

- Each row: a dot, the check's name, its status word with an optional detail. `passed` in `success-fg`, `changes to review` in `signal-text`, `pending` in `fg-muted`; the dot follows.
- The approve action is the one blue surface on the landing page: `action-text` with `action-fg`, 9.7:1 in Light and 12:1 in Graphite. It is a label of what the person does, not a working button on the page.
