## Guidelines

- **Kicker** in `meta` style, `action-text`; **heading** condensed, from the heading size on phones to `display-lg` on desktop, at most 16 characters wide; **lede** in `fg-muted`, 44 characters wide.
- **One link at most**, in `action-text` with a thin arrow, underlined only on hover and focus. Use it when the section points at something real (an example in the repository, a docs page), never as a second call to action.
- Left-aligned on the page measure; the section below starts after `space-10` on phones and `space-16` on desktop.
