## Guidelines

- **Kicker** (optional) in `meta` style, `action-text`; **heading** condensed, a step below the hero headline (80% of the heading size on phones, 80% of `display-lg` on desktop), at most 24 characters wide, lines balanced; **lede** in `fg-muted`, 44 characters wide, never larger than the hero's subline.
- **One link at most**, an [Arrow link](/design-system/components/arrow-link/). Use it when the section points at something real (an example in the repository, a docs page), never as a second call to action.
- Left-aligned on the page measure; the section below starts after `space-10` on phones and `space-16` on desktop.
