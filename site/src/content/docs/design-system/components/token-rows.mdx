<Examples component="token-rows" />

## Guidelines

- Three rows at most.
- The swatch is the colour itself, or the radius drawn as a cornered square.
- Rows 18 px apart so the outlines (4 px offset) have air; on phones the type drops to 12 px.
- Surface and text come from the example's tokens; only the outline is the design system's.
