The main navigation of a page. On tablets and desktops its links sit in a row, followed by the controls passed to it (the [theme switch](/design-system/components/theme-switch/)). Below 760px a burger button opens them as a full-screen menu.

<Examples component="nav" />

The examples on this page are wide, so they show the row; open the story of "Menu open on a phone" at phone width to see the menu.

## Guidelines

- Four links or fewer, labelled with the place they lead to ("Docs"), not an action. With so few links, tablets keep the row.
- The burger has no outline until it is hovered or focused, and turns into a cross while the menu is open. Its accessible name says what it does ("Menu", "Close menu").
- The menu is a disclosure that takes the whole screen: the page behind it does not scroll, focus stays inside it, Escape closes it and returns focus to the button, and so does following a link.
- Motion follows the rest of the site: the sheet wipes down, a redline draws under the header, the links rise one after another. Reduced motion shows the menu at once.
- Links and the button are at least 44px high, so they work as touch targets.
