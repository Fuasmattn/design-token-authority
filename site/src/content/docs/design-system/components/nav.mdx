The main navigation: links in a row followed by the controls passed to it ([icon buttons](/design-system/components/icon-button/), the [theme switch](/design-system/components/theme-switch/)); below 760px a burger opens a full-screen menu with the controls at the bottom.

<Examples component="nav" />

The examples are wide; open the "Menu open on a phone" story at phone width to see the menu.

## Guidelines

- Four links or fewer, named after where they lead ("Docs").
- The burger is an icon control like the [Icon button](/design-system/components/icon-button/): a `bg-hover` circle on hover and focus, a cross while open, named "Menu" / "Close menu".
- The open menu takes the screen: the page behind doesn't scroll, focus stays inside, Escape or a link closes it and returns focus to the button.
- **The current page:** pass `current: true`; its link gets `aria-current="page"` and an `action-bg` underline at `stroke-ink`, words stay `fg-default`. Section links (`#how`) are never current.
- Links and the button are at least `size-target` (44px) high.
- **Motion:** the sheet wipes down (`slow`, `enter`) and up (`base`, `exit`), links rise with `motion.stagger`; at once under reduced motion ([Motion](/design-system/motion/)).
