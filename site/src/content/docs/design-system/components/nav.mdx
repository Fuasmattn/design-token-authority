The main navigation of a page: its links in a row, and below 760px one **Menu** button that opens them as a list.

<Examples component="nav" />

## Guidelines

- Four links or fewer, labelled with the place they lead to ("Docs"), not an action.
- The menu is a disclosure: the button says whether it is open (`aria-expanded`), Escape closes it and returns focus to the button, and so does following a link or clicking outside.
- Links and the button are at least 44px high, so they work as touch targets.
- The open menu is a surface with a hairline border, not a shadow, like every panel in this system.
