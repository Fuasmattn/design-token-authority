Four variants for actions: primary (`action-bg`), outline (`stroke-ink` in `border-strong`), quiet (`stroke-hairline` in `border-default`, no fill) and link (underlined `action-bg` text).

<Examples component="button" />

## Guidelines

- One primary button per view. Outline for the secondary action next to it; link for tertiary.
- Quiet for the small action that repeats on every row of a list (the playground's Copy and Download next to each output file), so a column of them does not outweigh the content. Its border turns `border-strong` on hover.
- At least 48px tall (44px is the minimum on touch), `radius-md`, the `label` text style.
- Focus is a `stroke-focus` ring in `action-subtle`, never only a colour change.
- Labels are verbs or verb phrases: "See a review", not "Click here".
- With an `href` it renders a link, otherwise a `<button>`.
- **In a Preact island** (the playground): the styles live in `Button.css`, so island components render the same button with the class `dta-button` plus the variant (`primary`, `outline`, `quiet`, `link`) instead of re-styling their own. [Token input](/design-system/components/token-input/), [Source bar](/design-system/components/source-bar/), [Output options](/design-system/components/output-options/), [Output files](/design-system/components/output-files/), [Copy button](/design-system/components/copy-button/) and [CLI hand-off](/design-system/components/cli-handoff/) do; a compact row may lower the height to the 44 px touch minimum.
