Details of what was selected in a view, so the view behind stays in place: in the [playground](/playground/), a collection's tokens with their alias chains and values in the selected mode, a lint finding in full with the chain of its token, a token from the graph with its readings in every mode (as `dta token get` prints them), or an output file (or the generated `dta.config.ts`) as its text, with Copy and Download. A side panel next to the view from 1024 px of viewport width, a bottom sheet below.

## When to use

- For details of one row of a list or table that the visitor wants to read without losing their place: open, read, close, next row.
- Not for the views themselves (those are [Tabs](/design-system/components/tabs/)) and not for confirmations or forms; there are no modal dialogs for content that belongs on the page.

## Guidelines

- **One component, two presentations, one native `<dialog>`.** Beside the view it is shown non-modal (`show()`), in the flow and sticky while the page scrolls. As the sheet it is modal (`showModal()`): fixed to the bottom, focus stays inside, the page behind is inert and veiled with `bg-canvas` at 80 %. Crossing 1024 px while open switches the presentation and keeps it open.
- **Focus:** opening moves focus to the panel's title, so a screen reader reads what opened; closing (the close button, an [Icon button](/design-system/components/icon-button/) with a cross; Escape; or a tap on the sheet's backdrop) returns focus to the row that opened it. Escape closes the side panel too.
- **Look:** `bg-surface` with a `stroke-ink` outline in `border-strong` and `radius-lg` (it is the thing in focus); the sheet keeps the same complete outline (its side lines run down to the screen's edge), rounds only its top corners and shows a grab handle. It once had only a top rule on rounded corners, which tapered off down the sides; borders are complete or straight (principle 3). A mono kicker in `fg-subtle` (`Collection · brand · 0.94`), a mono title. A finding's kicker (`error · no-dangling-aliases`) carries its severity (`kickerSeverity`): the marker shape before it and the kicker in `status-error-text` or `status-warning-text` ([Status colours](/design-system/status-colours/)).
- **Content:** a meta line in `fg-muted`, optional actions (a file's [Copy button](/design-system/components/copy-button/) and Download, outline Buttons at 44 px), an optional mode switch (a segmented `bg-subtle` well with `aria-pressed` buttons), an optional message shown whole in mono (the finding as the CLI prints it), and lines: a token name, then its alias chain and value (`→ {Colors.Foundation.Blue.700}  #003f8a`) in `fg-muted`, with a small swatch for colours. Or a file's text (`code`): mono, as it is, every line whole (a line is never broken inside a token such as `var(--colors-brand-primary)`), under a hairline rule; a long line scrolls sideways inside the code's own box, never the page (TICKET-114, replacing 079's wrapping, which broke `var(--` on phones). Paths in the kicker, title, meta, message and lines break after a `/` or `.` and keep their segments whole ([Token path](/design-system/components/token-path/)). The lines and the code scroll inside the panel (a focusable, named region), the head stays.
- **A token** (from the [Graph canvas](/design-system/components/graph-canvas/) or the [Graph list](/design-system/components/graph-list/), TICKET-078): kicker `Token · color · Brand(Alias)`, the token's name, a meta line ("Every brand, as `dta token get` reads it; the graph shows BrandA · Desktop."), then one line per reading in the words of `dta token get`: the modes (`BrandA`, or `every mode`), the one the graph shows tagged `in view` in `action-text`, and the value and chain (`#3b8fd9  Colors.Brand.Accent → Colors.Foundation.Sky.500`), or `unresolved: …` with the error square, in `status-error-text` (a line's `severity`). Under them its names in code (`facts`: CSS `var(--colors-brand-accent)`, Tailwind `--color-brand-accent`, or the CLI's "Not in the build output…"), then its findings (`notes`: the marker and the kicker `error · no-dangling-aliases · BrandB` in the status colours, the message in mono).
- **Ways on (`links`):** outline Buttons at 44 px at the end of the body (full width in the sheet), above the step row. A finding about a token has "Show in graph": the Graph view with that token selected, its brand and breakpoint shown and its row in view (the hash records it, `#graph/<id>`). A token has "Show in lint" (its first finding, one in view first) and "Show BrandB" for a finding in a brand the graph does not show (the graph switches to it). Back and forth, the panel follows.
- The row that opened the panel is marked in the view (`aria-current`, tinted `action-subtle`); after "Show in graph" or "Show in lint" the row shown takes its place, so closing returns focus there.
- **Next in a list (`next`):** for a finding, a step row ends the body and stays there while the lines above it scroll: "Next finding" (outline [Button](/design-system/components/button/), 44 px, the full width of the sheet) and where you are, `2 of 3`, in mono `fg-muted`. It goes through every finding in the Lint view's order and wraps after the last; the view's marked row follows. Focus stays on the button, a polite status says what is shown now (`2 of 3: Colors.Surface.Muted`), and closing returns focus to the row of the finding shown last. Shown only when there is more than one.
- **Motion:** the side panel comes in from the right (`space-6` and a fade, `motion.duration.slow`, `motion.ease.enter`) while the view's column narrows; the sheet slides up from the bottom edge and its canvas backdrop fades in. Both leave the way they came (`base`, `exit`), with the content they showed. CSS only: `@starting-style` and discrete `display`/`overlay` transitions on the native `<dialog>`. After "Next finding" the new content fades in (`dta-appear`, `base`) while the head and the step row stay, and the position lights up ([Changed](/design-system/components/changed/)). Under reduced motion they appear and go at once (the position's highlight stays). See [Motion](/design-system/motion/).
