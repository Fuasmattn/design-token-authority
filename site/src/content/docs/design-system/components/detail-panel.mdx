Details of a selection without leaving the view: a side panel from 1024 px, a bottom sheet below. In the [playground](/playground/) it shows a collection's tokens, a lint finding, a token from the graph, or an output file.

## When to use

- For one row of a list or table that the visitor reads without losing their place.
- Not for views (those are [Tabs](/design-system/components/tabs/)), confirmations or forms.

## Guidelines

- One native `<dialog>`: non-modal beside the view (sticky while the page scrolls), modal as the sheet (focus kept inside, the page behind inert). Crossing 1024 px keeps it open.
- Opening moves focus to its title; closing (the close [Icon button](/design-system/components/icon-button/), Escape, or a tap on the sheet's backdrop) returns focus to the row that opened it.
- `bg-surface`, a complete `stroke-ink` outline in `border-strong`, `radius-lg`; the sheet rounds only its top corners and shows a grab handle.
- A finding's kicker carries its severity: the marker shape and the status text colour ([Status colours](/design-system/status-colours/)).
- Code shows every line whole and scrolls sideways inside its own box, never the page (wrapping would break `var(--` apart on phones). Paths break after `/` or `.`.
- The body scrolls inside the panel (a focusable, named region); the head stays.
- Ways on ("Show in graph", "Show in lint") are outline Buttons at 44 px, full width in the sheet.
- The row that opened it is marked in the view (`aria-current`, `action-subtle`).
- "Next finding" with its position (`2 of 3`) stays at the end of the body; a polite status says what is shown.
- Motion: the side panel comes in from the right, the sheet slides up; both leave the way they came. Under reduced motion they appear at once. See [Motion](/design-system/motion/).
