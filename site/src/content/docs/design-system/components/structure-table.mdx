The structure of a token set, as `analyzeStructure` in `@dta/core` infers it and `dta analyze` prints it: one row per collection with its modes, the inferred layer role (primitives, brand, dimension, semantic or unknown) and its confidence, the variable count and the share of aliases. Used by the [playground](/playground/).

## When to use

- To show what `analyzeStructure` found, wherever a token set has been read: the playground's Structure view.
- Selectable when there is more to see per collection (its tokens); plain in documentation.

## Guidelines

- Rules and type on the surface, no card: a 2 px ink rule under the mono header, hairlines between rows.
- Names, modes and numbers are mono; modes are small `bg-subtle` labels.
- A confidence below the review threshold (`LOW_CONFIDENCE`, the one `dta analyze` marks with ⚠) gets "⚠ review" in `status-warning-text` (a caveat, not a change: never `signal`; [Status colours](/design-system/status-colours/)): a word, not colour alone. Notes follow their row in `fg-muted`.
- In a container narrower than 640 px each collection becomes a short labelled list; the table keeps explicit roles for screen readers.
- The brands (the modes of the brand collection) go under the table when there are any.
- **Selectable rows** (`onSelect`): each collection name becomes a button with a `›`, and the whole row is its hit area. It opens the collection's details in the [Detail panel](/design-system/components/detail-panel/); the open row is marked with `aria-current` and tinted `action-subtle` (text in `action-text`), hovered rows `bg-subtle`. Inside a tab whose label already names the view, `hideCaption` keeps the caption for screen readers only.
- **Motion:** the selected row's tint comes and goes over `motion.duration.fast`; instant under reduced motion. See [Motion](/design-system/motion/).
