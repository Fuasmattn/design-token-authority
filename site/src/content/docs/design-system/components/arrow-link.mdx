The one style for "go there": a label in `action-text` and a thin arrow, underlined on hover and focus. Used by the section intro, the workflow and the final call on the landing page.

<Examples component="arrow-link" />

## Guidelines

- **Only for going to another page** (the reference, a guide, the docs). An action on the current page is a [Button](/design-system/components/button/).
- **The label names the destination**, not the action: "Command reference", "Coding agents", "Read the docs".
- Several in a row wrap with `space-3` between lines and `space-8` between links; stacked, they take `space-3`.
