A surface with a hairline border, the large radius and card padding. With `href` the whole card is a link that lifts on hover; `featured` draws it in the action colour; `grid` lays grid paper under the content.

<Examples component="card" />

## Guidelines

- One idea per card; cards in a row have the same structure.
- At most one featured card per group.
- A link card has one target: no other links inside it.
- Borders, not shadows: the card lifts on hover by moving and darkening its border.
- **Motion:** a link card lifts by `space-1` and its border turns ink over `motion.duration.base` on hover; still under reduced motion.
