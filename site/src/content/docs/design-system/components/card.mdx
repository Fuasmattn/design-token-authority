A surface for one idea, with a hairline border and the large radius. With `href` the whole card is a link; `featured` draws it in the action colour; `grid` lays grid paper under it.

<Examples component="card" />

## Guidelines

- One idea per card; cards in a row share their structure.
- At most one featured card per group.
- A link card has one target: no links inside it.
- Borders, not shadows: on hover a link card lifts by `space-1` and its border turns ink (`motion.duration.base`); still under reduced motion.
