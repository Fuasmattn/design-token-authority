One scene of the landing page's story: a numbered kicker, a heading, one paragraph, the command as a [command button](/design-system/components/command-button/) and the example in a slot. The example sits on the surface itself: no border, no card chrome.

<Examples component="scene" />

## Guidelines

- The kicker is `number · title` in mono weight 500 and `action-text` (AAA on the canvas in both themes).
- One heading, one paragraph (`fg-muted`, 44 characters wide at most), one command. Nothing numbered beyond the kicker.
- The example takes the right column by default (`example="right"`); scenes alternate with `example="left"`. On phones the copy comes first and the example stacks under it.
- Scenes are `space-36` apart on desktop; the page, not the scene, sets that.
