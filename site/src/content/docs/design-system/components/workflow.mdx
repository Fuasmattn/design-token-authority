## Guidelines

- **Four boxes, one sentence each.** A box is the command with its `$` prompt (`signal-text`, as on the canvas, not in a terminal) and one sentence about what it does for the user. The command is the title: no extra headings. Every sentence must be true for every user, as written in `--help`, the lint rules and the guides.
- **The person.** The last box is the person who decides, labelled in `action-text` and outlined in `border-strong` (`stroke-ink`); the boxes before it use the hairline in `border-default` on `bg-surface`.
- **Heading.** The section intro on top: the kicker is the anchor's name, the heading names the benefit.
- **One link** after the boxes, to where the commands are explained (the command reference).
- **Layout.** Two columns on phones, four from 960px of container. Commands never wrap.
- **No motion.**
