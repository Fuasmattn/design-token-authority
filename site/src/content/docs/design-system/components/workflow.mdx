## Guidelines

- **Three beats, one line.** Each beat is a mono eyebrow (where it happens), a title of a few words and two short sentences. No commands, no numbers: the example section below carries them. A faint dotted `rail` line (1px, `1 7`) runs through the three stations to the end.
- **Stations and the end.** Stations are 16px rings in `border-strong` that fill as the token passes and stay filled. The end is a ring in `action-bg` that fills, gets a soft halo and a tick when the token arrives: merged. The end state is labelled in `action-text`; the step back before it carries no label.
- **The token.** The hero's cube (`well-object-orange`, the same faces and hairline). It slows and breathes a little while it waits at a station; at the decision it steps back once and returns; it stays orange and full size at the end. Twelve motes of `well-dust` (two as the hero's spheres) flow along the line on their own, at their own pace, as long as the page is open, and bend toward the token when they pass near it.
- **Motion.** One run of 28 s, started once when a third of the section is visible; never loops. Positions are computed from the stations after layout, so the same component serves the stacked and the three-column layout. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the finished picture is shown. Transitions take the motion tokens.
- **Layout.** Stacked on phones with the line on the left; from 960px of container three columns on a horizontal line, the end to the right of the last column.
