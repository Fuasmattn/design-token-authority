Summarises one `dta diff` result: story and viewport, baseline / candidate / diff thumbnails, the change ratio against the threshold, and the action.

<Examples component="diff-card" />

## Guidelines

- The diff thumbnail uses `signal-bg` hatching over `signal-subtle`; the change bar fills with `signal-bg` relative to the threshold.
- The state chip says "review", "pass" or "blocked"; a blocked card says why in text.
- Thumbnails are placeholders until `dta diff` renders real screenshots.
- One primary action, "Approve change", only when there is something to review.
