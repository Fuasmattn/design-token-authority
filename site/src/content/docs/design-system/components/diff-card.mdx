One story × viewport of a `dta diff` report: the "after" screenshot with every changed element outlined and named, a table of the tokens, and "Show before". The [report](/guides/dta-diff-in-pull-requests/#reading-the-report) writes the same row.

<Examples component="diff-card" />

## Guidelines

- Outlines are `signal-bg` with a 1 px `ink-900` line inside and out: signal alone is under 3:1 on light screenshots. The ink is fixed `ink-900`, since screenshots don't follow the theme.
- Notes name the token and its change: colours as the alias step (`Nova 500 → 600`), other values as numbers (`8 → 24`).
- Nothing covers the story: a note sits on the image only over one flat colour; otherwise its box gets a numbered marker and the note is listed under the image. Boxes with the same token changes share one entry.
- Notes are redline labels (`signal-fg` on `signal-bg`), the design system's one AA exception (`data-contrast-exception`).
- "Show before" is a toggle button (`aria-pressed`, Enter and Space): it swaps the image in place and hides the outlines without moving anything.
- Sides in words: "With this change" and "Before this change", each with branch and short hash. Never "head" or "base" in anything a reader sees or hears.
- Unexplained rows show the diff overlay by default, a dashed box and a sentence on what that means.
- Status chips: "changed", then "explained" (neutral) or "unexplained" (error).
- Phone width: toggles full width, notes on two lines, the token table as a labelled list.
- Without JavaScript the image, outlines and links stay; notes are listed under the image.
