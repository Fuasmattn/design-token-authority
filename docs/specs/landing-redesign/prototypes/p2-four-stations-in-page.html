The deterministic backbone for design token workflows, built for humans and agents.

A design change in Figma becomes a pull request: validated, built, visually diffed and approved by the people who care about it.

npm create dtaRead the quick start

Design on one lane, product on the other.

Designers see their change in Figma at once. What they cannot see is what it does to the product that already exists: every brand, every state, every place a value was hard-coded.

DTA sits where the two meet, the pull request, and sends each fix back to the side that caused it.
