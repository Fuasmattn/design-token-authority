Decisions

Layers as columns. Primitives · Brand(Alias) · ScreenType, left to right, the selected brand and breakpoint in the headers. Arrows run from an alias to the token it uses, so every chain reads right to left.

Few crossings without a layout engine. Primitive groups are ordered by the layer that uses them (colours, effects, font families for Brand; spacing, radius, sizes for ScreenType), and ScreenType starts level with its targets, so its edges pass under the Brand column.

Nodes are rows on the canvas: a 1 px ink outline, the leaf name under a mono group label, the value with a swatch for colours. No cards, no shadows.

Findings keep shape + word + colour: a filled square and “error” on the node, its tint inside a complete 2 px marker outline; a circular pair gets two error arcs and a “circular” chip; a dangling alias points at a missing node. A file finding (default mode name) sits on its layer header.

Selection is blueprint: the token and every node on its chain on action-subtle with a 2 px action-bg outline, the chain’s edges 2.5 px.

Phones get the list: native selects for brand and breakpoint, layers as disclosures (summaries first), each token with its chain; the sheet is the same Detail panel.

Not signal. Nothing here is a change, so no redline orange (the swatch of Accent is a token’s value, not UI).
