# TICKET-121: Polish after the status colours (paths, selected finding, CopyButton, review colour)

**Phase:** 8 — Token Engine Overhaul, Epic 7b (playground)
**Priority:** Low
**Effort:** S

**Status:** in progress (items 1 and 3 done 2026-10-20; items 2 and 4 wait for the maintainer's decisions)

## What to build

Small things left open by 120 and noticed on its screenshots (2026-10-19):

1. **Token paths break at `/`, never mid-word.** The status banner (in `Findings.css`) breaks "Surface/Muted" as "Surface/Mu-ted" because the text uses `overflow-wrap: anywhere`. Token paths (and dotted alias ids) should break after a `/` or `.` and keep each segment whole; a single segment wider than its box may still break as a last resort. One shared way to render a path (e.g. a `<wbr>` after each separator, or a small `TokenPath` helper in `site/src/components/ds`), used everywhere a path can wrap: the banner, `Findings` lines and details, `DetailPanel`, `OutputFiles`, `SourceBar`, `CliHandoff`.
2. **A selected finding keeps its severity marker (decision needed).** Today the selected line turns label, detail and marker to `action-text`, so a selected error loses its `status-error-marker` square. Proposal: the row keeps the action tint (selection) but the marker keeps its status colour and shape, so selection and severity are both visible at once. Alternative: keep today's behaviour (selection wins). **Decided (maintainer, 2026-10-20): the proposal** — the marker keeps its status colour and shape on a selected row. Contrast of the status marker on `action-subtle` must be checked (3:1 for the non-text marker, both themes).
3. **CopyButton "Not copied" gets the error marker.** "Copied" has the pass dot in `status-success-marker`; "Not copied" has none, so the failure relies on the word alone in a spot where success has a shape. Add the error square in `status-error-marker` (shape + word, not colour alone); the shared grid cell keeps the width stable.
4. **"Review" vs "changes to review" (decision needed).** The `dta diff` report's "review" verdict uses the action tint (`packages/visual-diff/src/report/html.ts`, `VERDICT`), Checks' "changes to review" uses `signal`. Both mean "a diff a person looks at" and are deliberately not statuses (120). Proposal: the report follows Checks and uses `signal` (signal orange is for changes/diffs, which this is); the action tint stays for actions. Alternative: keep both and record why they differ.

- **1. Paths break between segments: `TokenPath` (new design-system component).** `site/src/components/ds/TokenPath.tsx`: `pathParts(text)` cuts a text after each separator a path may break at, `breakPaths(text)` puts a `<wbr>` there, `<TokenPath path>` is the same as a mono `span.dta-path` (`TokenPath.css`: `font-family: var(--font-mono)`, `overflow-wrap: anywhere`) for a path on its own. **Rule (decision):** a break after `/` between two non-space characters (not `//`), and after `.` that follows a letter or `)`/`]`/`}`, or a digit when no digit follows (`Mode 1.json`); never in a decimal (`0.94`, `1.2 KB`), at a full stop before a space or at the end, or in `...`. It runs on whole lines of prose, so a diagnostic's paths break while its sentences stay as they are. **Why `<wbr>` and keep `overflow-wrap: anywhere`:** `anywhere` only applies when a line has no other break opportunity, so with a `<wbr>` after every separator it is left for a single segment wider than its box (the last resort the ticket allows); the components' min-content sizing is unchanged. `<wbr>` adds no text, so copied text, accessible names and every text assertion stay the same.
